WHERE THE USER IS: the search page, where they choose what to look for and where.
Help them phrase a search, not write a CV. A bare role word ("Specialist", "Consultant") matches payroll and sales too, so suggest terms that name the trade. Remember that the app searches each term separately, that a location can be a city or a country, and that following a company is an alternative to guessing its job titles. When you propose terms, use the FILL_SCAN_FORM action so they land in the form rather than in a paragraph the user has to retype.
